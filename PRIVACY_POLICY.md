# Privacy Policy — The Milton Paper

**Last updated: 30 September 2026**

The Milton Paper is a student newspaper at Milton Academy. This policy explains
what our iPhone app collects, why, and what you can do about it. We have tried
to write it in plain English rather than legal language.

If anything here is unclear, email **themiltonpaper40@gmail.com**.

## The short version

You can read the entire paper without an account, and we do not sell anything
about you to anyone. Signing in is optional, and only exists so your saved
stories follow you between devices. Your reading history never leaves your
phone.

## What we collect

**If you don't sign in**, we collect no personal information. The app fetches
articles the same way a web browser does. Your saved stories and reading
history stay on your device.

**If you sign in with Google**, we store:

| What | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Your email address | To identify your account |
| Your name, as Google provides it | So it can be shown on your profile |
| A unique account ID | To link your saved stories to you |
| The date you joined | Account housekeeping |
| Which stories you've saved | So they appear on your other devices |
| Which notification topics you chose | So we send only what you asked for |

**If you play TMPlay**, and only if you are signed in, we store your score for
that day: your display name, the number of guesses, and how long you took.

**Please note: the TMPlay leaderboard is public.** Anyone using the app can see
the display name and score of everyone on it. If you would rather not appear
there, don't sign in while playing — the games work perfectly well signed out,
they just won't be ranked.

**Automatically**, the app uses Google Firebase, which collects standard
analytics such as device type, app version, rough region, and which screens are
opened. This is aggregate information about usage, not a record of what any one
person read.

## What stays on your phone and never reaches us

- **Your reading history** — which articles you opened and how far you got.
  This powers "Continue reading" and is stored only on your device. We cannot
  see it, and it is deleted when you delete the app.
- **Cached articles and images**, so the app works offline.

## What we never collect

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera. We
have no advertising and no third-party ad trackers. We do not sell, rent or
trade your information, and we never will.

## Who else is involved

- **Google Firebase** stores accounts, saved stories and leaderboard scores, and
  provides analytics. Google's privacy policy applies to that processing:
  https://policies.google.com/privacy
- **Google Sign-In** handles logging in. We never see your Google password.
- **themiltonpaper.com** serves the articles and images the app displays.

## Students in our coverage

This policy covers the app. Separately, if you are a Milton student, parent or
teacher and you want your name, photo or a quotation removed from a story we
have published, email **themiltonpaper40@gmail.com** and we will take it
seriously. We would rather correct the record than defend it.

## Children

The app is intended for the Milton Academy community and readers aged 13 and
over. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you
believe a child under 13 has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

## Deleting your data

In the app, go to **You → Delete account**. This removes your profile, your
saved stories and your leaderboard scores from our database. Reading history is
on your device, so deleting the app removes it.

You can also just email us and we'll do it for you.

## Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date at the top. If a change is
significant, we'll say so in the app.
